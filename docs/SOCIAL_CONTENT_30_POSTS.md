# Lading - 30 Short-Video Posts

Ready-to-produce short-form video posts for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Threads and Bluesky. Each post uses the Scroll Stoppers hook system and an INTERRUPT pattern interrupt, and follows the standard scene format.

Production notes:
- Vertical 9:16, 15-40 seconds, captions burned in, sound-off safe.
- One idea per video. Product screen recordings convert best.
- Add the guidance-only disclaimer in the caption or as a pinned comment.
